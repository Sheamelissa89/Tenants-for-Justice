Why this exists

Clear records help people be heard.

Tenants for Justice was created to help renters transform scattered messages, photographs, notices, reports, and memories into an organized case record.

Built for tenants—and future collaboration

The long-term vision is a secure platform where individuals can prepare their records and, when they choose, collaborate efficiently with qualified legal professionals.

Important Legal Disclaimer

This application provides organizational tools and general information. It is not a law firm, does not provide legal advice, and does not create an attorney-client relationship.
