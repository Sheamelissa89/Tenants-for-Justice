Reliable starting points

Tenant resources

Use official and nonprofit sources to understand options. Confirm deadlines and legal requirements with a qualified professional.

Urgent danger

Call 911 for immediate danger. Contact the appropriate local emergency or code-enforcement agency for urgent property conditions.

Legal help

Look for a licensed housing attorney or local legal-aid organization. Bring your timeline, notices, communications, photographs, reports, lease, and payment records.

Documentation

Record dates, exact statements, witnesses, delivery methods, and follow-up. Preserve original files and avoid altering source evidence.
